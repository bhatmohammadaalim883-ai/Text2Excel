How to use Text2Excel

Text to Excel

  1. Paste your text or upload a .txt or .csv file.
  2. Choose how columns should be separated.
  3. Check the spreadsheet preview.
  4. Download your Excel workbook.

Excel to Text

  1. Open the Excel to Text tab.
  2. Upload your .xlsx or .xls workbook.
  3. Choose a worksheet to preview.
  4. Select Plain Text, CSV, JSON or Markdown.
  5. Click Convert all sheets.
  6. Copy or download the converted output.

Frequently asked questions

Is Text2Excel free?

Yes. The converter is free to use.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion takes place locally in your browser. Your files are not sent to our server by the conversion code.

Can I convert Excel to JSON?

Yes. You can convert all worksheets into a JSON object, with each worksheet represented separately.

Can I convert multiple Excel sheets?

Yes. All worksheets in the uploaded workbook are imported and converted.

Which Excel formats are supported?

The tool accepts .xlsx, .xls, .xlsm and .xlsb files supported by the SheetJS library.

Built with SheetJS. All conversion happens in your browser.
